About TopZeroTurnMower.com

Who runs this site, why we built it, and how we make our picks.

Who We Are

TopZeroTurnMower.com is an independent review site run by homeowners who maintain large residential properties. We are not a manufacturer, a dealer, or a magazine. We're people who mow our own yards and got tired of review sites that treat every machine like a winner.

Our audience is the homeowner with 1–5 acres who mows weekly, takes the investment seriously, and wants to know which machine will still be running in 10 years — not which one has the best marketing photos.

How We Pick

We evaluate zero turn mowers on five criteria: build quality (fabricated vs. stamped, weld quality, hardware grade), engine reliability (brand, documented longevity, parts availability), cut quality across grass types and conditions, ease of long-session use (seat, controls, fatigue), and dealer/service support.

No manufacturer pays us to write reviews. We use Amazon affiliate links, which pay us a small commission if you buy through them — at no cost to you. That's how the site sustains itself.
