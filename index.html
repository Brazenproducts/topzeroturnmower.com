Why Zero Turn?

If you're mowing more than an acre, a riding mower is a frustrating compromise. Zero turn radius means you're not doing three-point turns around every tree, fence post, and flower bed. A good zero turn will cut your mowing time by 30–40% compared to a comparable rider — and it'll look better doing it.

The trade-off: they're not great on steep slopes (serious hills need a different approach), and they cost more up front. But for flat to moderately hilly terrain, they're the obvious choice once you've used one.

Here's our honest take on which brands actually deliver — and which ones coast on name recognition.

The Brands Worth Knowing

🟠

Husqvarna

Swedish engineering, built in the US. Consistent quality, dealer support, and parts availability. The Z254F is their sweet spot.

🔴

Toro

TimeCutter series punches above its weight on price. Ironically popular in commercial settings for residential-grade machines.

🟡

Cub Cadet

Ultima ZT1 is the overlooked middle ground. Good cut quality, decent build, lower profile than Husqvarna and Toro.

🟢

John Deere

Z335E is their entry-level zero turn. Overpriced for what it is, but the dealer network and resale value are unmatched.
